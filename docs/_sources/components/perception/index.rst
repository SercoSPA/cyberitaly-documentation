CyberItaly Visualization and Analytics
=================

*CyberItaly Visualization and Analytics* is available at https://iride-cyberitaly.space/perception.

A key feature of CyberItaly is the ability to find, explore and use a range of the outputs of the Digital Twins runs and scenarios, interrogate and analyse them.

CyberItaly Visualization and Analytics allows users to discover Digital Twins data and exploit them with advanced analytics.

The images below provide just a few examples of how different type of data (such raster, vector, time series, and volumetric data) can be exploited.
